Role & responsibilities

We are seeking a Network Implementation Engineer to perform the staging, configuration, migration, and cutover of enterprise and data center network environments. This role is responsible for turning Low-Level Designs (LLDs) and High-Level Designs (HLDs) into production reality across Cisco routing, switching, wireless, and security ecosystems. The ideal candidate has deep Cisco CLI expertise, hands-on experience executing maintenance windows, and familiarity with Cisco's controller-led architectures (Catalyst Center).


Key Roles and Responsibilities


Implement and configure Router and Switches in enterprise environment.
Replacement and migration of EOL router and Switches in enterprise setup.
Understating and experience on ACL and Qos configuration.
Bootstrap and apply golden configurations across Cisco Catalyst (9200/9300/9400/9500/9600) and ASR/ISR/Catalyst 8000,4300,1100 series routers.
Provision Cisco Catalyst Wireless infrastructure, including 9800,5520,8500 Series Wireless LAN Controllers (WLCs) and Wi-Fi 6/6E/7 Access Points (APs).
Onboard devices into Cisco Catalyst Center (formerly DNA Spaces/DNAC) for automated template deployment.
Lead scheduled, off-hours maintenance windows to perform core/distribution cutovers, legacy hardware decommissioning, and software upgrades (IOS-XE / NX-OS).
Run post-cutover operational verification: ARP/MAC table validation, OSPF/BGP neighbor adjacency checks, STP root bridge verification, and interface error monitoring.
Implement and configure APs and WLC in enterprise environment.
Perform AP onboarding, provisioning and configuration on WLC.
Configure SSID, WLAN, Vlan Mapping and Wireless authentication policies.
Configure AP groups, RF profile, Power Settings, Mobility and roaming parameters.
Perform WLC Software upgrade, SSL certificate installation.

The skills Required


Routing- Strong knowledge and hands-on experience in BGP and OSPF.
Knowledge on MPLS will be a advantage.
Switching- Deep understanding on switching concept like STP, HSRP, VRRP, Portchannel, Switch Stacking, VTP.
Wireless- Hands on experience on cisco air OS and catalyst controllers (8500,5520,9800), Access point, RF fundamentals, WLAN fundamentals.

Experience & Certifications


Experience: 6 months of hands-on deployment and migration experience specifically within production networks.
Education Graduate from any stream (Engineering graduates preferred) or equivalent practical field experience.
Certifications (Required or Strongly Preferred):
Cisco CCNA (minimum prerequisite if accompanied by deep field experience),CCNP preferred.
Cisco CCNA Wireless is a strong plus.
Participation in off-hours change windows (nights and weekends) to minimize operational disruption.
